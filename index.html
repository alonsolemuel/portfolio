FarmLink

Fresh from the farm,
straight to your table.

A direct marketplace where local vegetable farmers sell to households, restaurants and vendors, without losing most of their earnings to middlemen.

🌱Farmer lists produce
🛒Buyer orders online
🚚Fresh delivery
01 · Problem

Farmers earn too little

Small farmers usually sell through middlemen who set the price. Buyers in the city can't easily buy direct, and unsold vegetables go to waste.

02 · Solution

A direct, simple marketplace

Farmers post their harvest with price and quantity from their phone. Buyers browse, order and pay in one place, and farmers get a fairer share.

03 · Who it's for

Two sides of the market

Sellers: small vegetable farmers in Mantalongon.
Buyers: households, carinderias, restaurants and market vendors in Cebu.

04 · Business model

How it makes money

  • Small commission per order
  • Delivery fee
  • Featured listings for farmers
  • Bulk-order plan for restaurants
